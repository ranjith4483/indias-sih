    Executive Summary & MoES Alignment

    Commercial open-ocean Argo floats cost between $25,000 and $100,000+ (₹20L–₹80L+ INR) per unit and weigh 20–35 kg, requiring large research vessels and crane rigs. Furthermore, conventional floats lack active acoustic collision avoidance, rendering them vulnerable to destruction against overhead sea ice sheets during polar ascents.

    INDIA'S SIH solves Ministry of Earth Sciences Problem Statement SIH26065 by providing an indigenous, ultra-low-cost, modular autonomous profiling float achieving an ~90% cost reduction. It features an active upward-facing ultrasonic sonar (JSN-SR04T) that triggers an automatic Ice Abort Procedure within 3m of overhead ice, and a COTS sliding internal chassis enabling single-operator hand launches.

    Key Subsystems & Engineering "Why"

    Buoyancy Engine

    100m: NEMA 17 + T8 Lead Screw Syringe (±51.4g force)

    Why: Provides precise linear volume displacement (100 mL) for shallow profiling without expensive pumps.

    Pressure Hull Structure

    100m: Schedule 80 PVC (8.43x Safety Factor)

    Why: Extremely cost-effective, easy to machine, and withstands 10.08 Bar hydrostatic pressure with zero wall deformation.

    Ice Avoidance Sonar

    JSN-SR04T Waterproof Ultrasonic Sensor

    Why: Mounted facing upward to detect overhead polar ice sheets within 3m, triggering an emergency sink abort.

    Sensor Data Sampling

    Passive-Flow Hydrostatic Architecture

    Why: Water flows naturally past flush-mounted external ports during vertical drift, eliminating power-hungry internal pumps.
